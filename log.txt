0. WHERE WE STAND IN ONE PARAGRAPH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The LGS paper was rejected (see improvements.txt). LGS cannot be defended: its
lead-bias prior contributes ~+.005 held-out, its closest prior art (SummaC,
Chen et al. 2021) is uncited, and a 4-line lead-window heuristic matches it on
the benchmark's own axis. This session built and evaluated FIVE new metric
ideas end-to-end on the full SummEval set. Three failed, two worked. The
current best candidate is LNC-fast: a per-dimension ridge combination of cheap
reference-free signals, 227 ms/sample on the GB10, which is Pareto-optimal on
both the raw and the extractiveness-partialled axis when every GPU metric is
timed on the same machine. Its novelty is empirical, not a new primitive.
The biggest remaining hole is that its calibration is fitted on SummEval only
(no second corpus).

1. EVALUATION PROTOCOL USED THROUGHOUT (keep doing this)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Two axes, always reported together:
    raw ρ̄      = mean summary-level Spearman over the 4 SummEval dimensions
    partial ρ̄  = same with the bigram copy rate partialled out (cmd/confound)
    ρ_copy     = the metric's own correlation with the copy rate
  The partial axis exists because SummEval's ratings are heavily confounded
  with extractiveness (improvements.txt §2.4). NOTE: Durmus, Ladhak,
  Hashimoto (ACL 2022) already published this for the CONSISTENCY dimension —
  cite them and differentiate (all four dimensions, partial correlations,
  LLM judges).
- Article-level splits by dataset order: dev = first 50 articles, test = last
  50. Every design decision (which signal, which variant, ridge vs selection,
  calibration weights) is made on dev ONLY and verified once on test.
- For any metric calibrated on dev, paired tests must run on the test half:
    go run ./cmd/compare -metric <name> -doc-split last50 ...
  (-doc-split was added this session.)
- Report random-split robustness, not just the canonical split. The JSONL-order
  split turned out to be mildly favourable for LNC (.354 test vs .331 mean over
  200 random 50/50 article splits). Quote the random-split number.
- Cost claims are only valid on one machine. See §4.

2. WHAT WAS BUILT AND WHAT HAPPENED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Numbers are full-set partial ρ̄ unless marked. All code is regenerable via make
targets; every run wrote per-sample dumps so analyses can be redone without a
GPU.

2.1 CCM — Copy-invariant Contrastive Margin   (pkg/metrics/ccm.go, cmd/ccm)
    score = log P(y|x) − mean_k log P(y'_k|x), Qwen2.5-1.5B, K=8 local
    perturbations of the candidate (entity swap / number change / negation
    flip / sentence swap). Hypothesis: perturbations preserve the copy rate,
    so the copy bonus cancels.
    RESULT: FAILED. raw .225, partial .124, ρ_copy .43 — worse than LGS.
    WHY: with the source in context the LM predicts verbatim-copied spans
    almost deterministically, so perturbing a copied span costs MORE
    likelihood than perturbing a paraphrase. The margin rewards copying.
    Entity/number perturbations carry almost no consistency signal (~.10).
    Closest prior art: DeltaScore (Findings EMNLP 2023) has the same formula
    with one perturbation for story evaluation; DetectGPT/Fast-DetectGPT the
    K-perturbation curvature. MUST be cited.

2.2 CCM-D — per-dimension CCM (same run, no extra model calls)
    One dev-selected signal per dimension from a fixed pool of 7.
    RESULT: partial .290 full / .297 test, raw .388. Superseded by LNC.

2.3 PSC — Paraphrased-Source Conditioning   (pkg/metrics/psc.go, cmd/psc)
    Same signals but conditioned on x̃, an LLM paraphrase of the source
    (qwen2.5:7b-instruct, 3-sentence chunks, T=0, cached in
    ablation/psc_paraphrases.json).
    RESULT: mechanism CONFIRMED, metric WORSE. ρ_copy of the likelihood drops
    .74 → .18; the paraphrase shares 21% of bigrams with the source; the
    per-summary diagnostic (log P(y|x) − log P(y|x̃))/|y| correlates .57 with
    copy rate. But partial drops .290 → .257 (test .297 → .239): removing the
    copy bonus also removed real signal, and the paraphrase adds noise
    (observed drifts, e.g. an invented "During an earlier part of the season").
    Prior art: paraphrasing the REFERENCE is known (Freitag et al. 2020,
    Para-Ref NAACL 2024); Prism (EMNLP 2020) documents the copy bonus in
    likelihood scoring; Dreyer et al. (EACL Findings 2023) adjust factuality
    metrics for abstractiveness post hoc. Paraphrasing the CONDITIONING SOURCE
    appears unpublished — this is the most defensible novel mechanism found,
    even though it does not win as a metric.

2.4 NLIG — NLI grounding   (pkg/metrics/nlig.go, cmd/nlig)
    SummaC-ZS construction: mean over summary sentences of
    max over 1- and 2-sentence source windows of P(entail) − P(contradict),
    DeBERTa-v3-large MNLI/FEVER/ANLI/LingNLI/WANLI.
    RESULT: the best consistency signal in the whole pool — consistency
    partial .362 full, .413 test (UniEval .289, G-Eval .398, CCM-D .237).
    Other dimensions weak; mean partial .189. NOT NOVEL (SummaC, TACL 2022);
    use it as a component and as the answer to "cosine is not entailment".

2.5 SPL — Splice-Point Likelihood   (pkg/metrics/spl.go, cmd/spl)
    Motivated by the fact that the MEDIAN SummEval candidate copies 92% of its
    bigrams: score only the words where the candidate departs from a verbatim
    source fragment (first word of each fragment + words not continuing a
    source bigram).
    RESULT: FAILED. raw .123, partial .139. The words INSIDE copied fragments
    carry more signal (partial .197). Verified not to be a bug: per-word
    log-probs sum back to CCM's sequence log-prob within 0.03 nats.

2.6 LNC — Likelihood–NLI Composite   (pkg/metrics/lnc.go, cmd/lnc)  ** BEST **
    10 features, all reference-free, from the runs above:
      CCM:  logp_tok, logp_sum, ulogp_tok, order margin, negation margin, zmargin
      NLIG: mean, min
      SPL:  splice, inside-copy
    Per-dimension ridge (α=10 fixed a priori) on standardised features, fitted
    on dev only, frozen in ablation/lnc_calibration.json.
    Design decision made on dev ONLY: ridge beat per-dimension single/pair
    selection by 5-fold article-level CV inside dev (.301 vs .295).
    A population-dependent rank feature was dropped so the score is computable
    per sample.
    RESULT (test half): raw .437, partial .354 (coh .375 / con .360 / flu .333
    / rel .347). Random splits: partial .331 [.302, .356], raw .420.
    Paired bootstrap on the test half, BH-FDR over 32 cells: 18 wins, 13 ties,
    1 loss (UniEval on coherence). Beats UniEval on CONSISTENCY (+.104), beats
    G-Eval on fluency and ties it elsewhere, beats CCM-D / LGS / BERTScore /
    GPTScore / BARTScore / Lead-5 broadly.
    Feature-group ablation (ridge refit on dev, mean over 100 random splits):
      all features                      .331
      without perturbation features     .278   <-- biggest single contribution
      without NLI                       .312   (NLI buys +.019 for 469 ms)
      without SPL                       .328   (SPL buys +.003)
      likelihood only                   .258
    So CCM failed as a metric but its perturbation features are the most
    valuable ingredient of the composite. That is a usable narrative.

2.7 LNC-D — debiased variant (make benchmark-lncd, no model calls)
    Same features, ridge target = human rank with the copy-rate rank projected
    out (copy rate used ONLY in fitting, never as a feature).
    RESULT: same partial (.354 test) with ρ_copy .25 instead of .55; raw falls
    to .398. Use it for the confound argument, not as the headline.

2.8 LNC-fast — the headline candidate (make benchmark-lncf)
    Identical pipeline and protocol; three cost cuts fixed a priori (NOT
    tuned): CCM LM in bf16 (CCM_DTYPE=bfloat16), K=4 instead of 8, NLI only
    against each summary sentence's 4 highest-overlap source windows
    (-nli-topk 4). Calibration refitted on dev.
    RESULT: 227 ms/sample (CCM 118 + NLI 85 + SPL 25) vs 854 for LNC.
    Test: raw .433, partial .350. Random splits: partial .323 [.298, .347].
    Paired bootstrap on test half (36 cells): 17 wins, 18 ties, 1 loss
    (UniEval coherence); ties full LNC on every dimension.
    PARETO: with same-hardware costs it is on the frontier on BOTH axes and is
    the only reference-free metric on the frontier above .2 partial, sitting
    between BERTScore (210 ms, .241) and UniEval (336 ms, .424).

5. NOVELTY STATUS (checked by literature search this session)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Perturbation margin: DeltaScore (Findings EMNLP 2023) = same formula, stories,
  one perturbation. DetectGPT / Fast-DetectGPT = curvature framing. CITE BOTH.
- Likelihood signals: BARTScore s→h, GPTScore, FFLM (EMNLP 2023), HaRiM+
  (AACL 2022), CPMI, Shannon Score. CITE; do not claim these as new.
- NLI grounding: SummaC-ZS/Conv (TACL 2022), AlignScore, MiniCheck, QAFactEval.
  Salience weighting: SUPERT (ACL 2020), Chen et al. (ACL 2021). LGS itself
  must cite SummaC and Chen — that is the uncited-prior-art hole reviewers hit.
- Learned metric combination: MetaMetrics (ICLR 2025). LNC is in this family.
- Sentence-order contrast for coherence: DeltaScore, Laban et al. (ACL 2021);
  Steen & Markert (COLING 2022) already showed shuffle-based measures transfer
  poorly to SummEval coherence — a negative precedent reviewers will know.
- Extractiveness confound: Durmus et al. (ACL 2022) for consistency only;
  Ladhak et al. (ACL 2022); Dreyer et al. (EACL Findings 2023).
- APPEARS UNPUBLISHED: conditioning likelihood metrics on a PARAPHRASED source,
  and the copy-advantage diagnostic log P(y|x) − log P(y|x̃).
Caveat: searches were engine-based, abstracts mostly; verify the few papers you
lean on by reading them.

7. OPS GOTCHAS (cost me time; will cost you too)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Background processes die between tool calls in this harness. Start the model
  server and the benchmark in ONE backgrounded command, not two.
- The machine had ~70 GB RAM in use by other processes; a full (batch, length,
  vocab) logits tensor in fp32 OOM-killed the server (and probably killed two
  sessions). /ccm now projects through the LM head CCM_HEAD_ROWS rows at a
  time and uses logits_to_keep=1 for the prefix pass. Peak is ~9 GB, of which
  6 GB is fp32 weights.
- fp32 weights + TF32 matmuls: batched-and-padded scoring matches a plain full
  forward to ~0.003 nats. Pure bf16 drifts ~0.1 nats, which is the size of
  small margins — that is why fp32 is the default and bf16 is opt-in for
  LNC-fast (where the ridge absorbs it; verified by the LNC-fast numbers).
- pkill -f <pattern> matches the agent's own shell command line and kills the
  session. Filter by /proc/<pid>/comm instead.
- All runs are deterministic: a rerun of cmd/ccm reproduced output/ccm.json
  exactly (max diff 0.0), and cmd/lnc -features-in reproduces LNC bit-for-bit.
