MILESTONES -- ordered by what blocks resubmission
================================================================================

M1. [BLOCKING] Baseline comparisons are confounded, not just under-budget.
    We independently verified this is worse than reviewers thought: a trivial
    lead-window ROUGE-L control (no model, no reference) MATCHES OR BEATS LGS
    on the paper's own mean-Spearman axis at ~1000x lower cost. The
    Pareto-optimality headline claim is false once this control is included.
    (Reviewer #3 M1, #5 #11, #4 cost-only-wall-clock point)
    -> improvements.txt section 2.3, 2.4. Must be fixed before resubmission,
       not just disclosed.

M2. [BLOCKING] Novelty vs. closer prior art is unverified.
    Related work skips the pre-LLM reference-free family (SUPERT, BLANC,
    SummaQA, ESTIME) and MetaMetrics (arXiv:2410.02381), which already reports
    runtime alongside correlation -- undercutting the "no prior meta-evaluation
    puts these on one axis" claim.
    (Reviewer #3 M4, Reviewer #4 related-work gap)
    -> gates everything else; if SUPERT already does "reference-free
       sentence-level grounding against source", LGS has no novelty
       independent of the M1 problem.

M3. [BLOCKING] lambda* (the paper's namesake contribution) is not
    identifiable from 50 dev articles: dev-optimal (0.5) != test-optimal
    (0.25) on the same backbone, and the cross-backbone "shift" is not
    separable from ordinary resampling noise.
    (Reviewer #3 M2, #4 lambda instability, #5 #7)
    -> partially measured (improvements.txt 2.1); "some prior beats none" is
       robust, "which exponent" is not. Needs a per-dimension decision matrix
       or an honest statement that the value is an interval, not a point.

M4. [MAJOR] "Strictly dominates BERTScore" overstates per-dimension evidence:
    only 1 of 4 dimensions (consistency) is significant; relevance numerically
    favors BERTScore.
    (Reviewer #3 M3)
    -> abstract still leads with the strong claim; needs rewording throughout,
       not just a caveat buried in 6.3.

M5. [MAJOR] Cosine similarity is conflated with factual grounding/entailment.
    The paper's own numbers agree: consistency (the factuality dimension) is
    LGS's worst result. This is the most promising direction for follow-up
    work (NLI-based consistency signal), not just a wording fix.
    (Reviewer #4, #6)

M6. [MAJOR] Single corpus only (SummEval / CNN-DailyMail), so the lead-bias
    premise itself is untested outside news, where no comparable positional
    prior may exist.
    (Reviewer #4, #6)
    -> biggest open item; a second corpus (e.g. Newsroom vs. arXiv/PubMed or
       SAMSum) is the only experiment that could rehabilitate the lead-bias
       prior as a genuine contribution rather than a dataset artifact.

M7. [MAJOR] Deployment scenarios (online model selection, release gating, RL
    reward modeling) are described with zero supporting experiments or
    simulations.
    (Reviewer #4 section 8.1, #5 #8)

M8. [MODERATE] Statistical rigor gaps:
    - No correction for multiple comparisons across the baseline table.
      (Reviewer #5 #9) -- FIXED: Benjamini-Hochberg FDR added, all 8
      significant verdicts survive (improvements.txt 2.2).
    - No Friedman/Nemenyi/Wilcoxon across the full metric pool.
      (Reviewer #1 #5, echoed by #5) -- NOT YET DONE. Correct blocking
      structure: 100 articles as blocks, metrics as treatments, per-article
      Spearman as the response variable per dimension.
    - Dev/test split by raw JSONL order is unjustified; no test of split
      sensitivity. (Reviewer #3 m1) -- cheap to fix, per-sample scores are
      already stored.

M9. [MINOR, mechanical] Table 1's "Prior"/"Ref."/"Judge" columns are
    self-referential and not self-explanatory (Reviewer #3 m2, #5 #4).
    Add: notation table, related-work pros/cons table, updated preprint
    citations, AI-editing-tool disclosure, recent (2025-2026) references,
    remove em dashes, tighten length (paper grew 63->69 pages in the last
    revision -- wrong direction), merge intro subsections, contributions as
    3-4 bullets with no internal cross-references.
    (Reviewer #5 #1/#3/#5/#13, folded-in generic asks from #1)

M10. [MINOR] Imprecise claims to reword, no new experiments needed:
    - "zero inference cost" for the prior -- it still costs arithmetic, just
      no extra forward pass. (Reviewer #4)
    - "negligible" overhead at ~57ms/sample -- not negligible at scale
      (thousands-millions of candidates). (Reviewer #4, #5)
    - Interpretability via argmax anchors is claimed with no human evaluation
      or case studies of anchor quality. (Reviewer #5 #10)

(Pareto-optimality, backbone-dependent recalibration) directly and both

measurement/confound paper plus a redesigned metric targeting the
partial-correlation axis) before spending time on M4-M10 polish.
